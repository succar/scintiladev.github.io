The SOS App

Personal safety application

The SOS App ("the App") is a personal safety application operated by The Drone Wave LLC, doing business as Scintilla Dev ("we," "us," or "our"). This section explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use The SOS App.

Your safety is our priority — and so is your privacy. We collect only what is necessary to deliver the core safety features you rely on.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

We collect the following information when you create or update your account:

  • Email address
  • Full name
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Locale, timezone, and language preference
  • Subscription plan (if applicable)

Users may update their name, phone number, and profile information at any time inside the App.

1.2 Location Data

We collect:

  • Precise GPS location — only when SOS tracking or family live‑location sharing is active
  • Approximate location — used as fallback when GPS is unavailable (IP‑based geolocation)

Your location is only shared with contacts YOU choose, such as emergency contacts or mutually approved family members.

1.3 Device Information

We collect limited device information to ensure the App functions properly:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging token (for push notifications)
  • Device model and OS version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostics (testing builds only; not collected in production)

We do not collect advertising identifiers.

1.4 Usage Information

We may collect anonymized or aggregated data about:

  • Feature usage
  • App interactions
  • Settings and preferences

This helps us improve reliability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Send SOS alerts to your chosen contacts
  • Share your live GPS location during emergencies
  • Enable family location sharing with mutual consent
  • Deliver push notifications (SOS alerts, check‑ins, family requests)
  • Authenticate and manage your account
  • Improve App performance and safety features
  • Prevent fraud or misuse

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information.

3. How We Share Your Information

We only share your data with:

3.1 Your Emergency Contacts

When SOS is triggered, your real‑time location and alert information are shared with the contacts you designate.

3.2 Family Members (Mutual Consent Required)

Both parties must approve the connection before any location data is shared. Either party can remove the connection at any time.

3.3 Service Providers

We use trusted third‑party services to power core functionality:

Supabase — Database, authentication, real‑time servicesPrivacy Policy ↗
Firebase Cloud Messaging — Push notificationsPrivacy Policy ↗
Mapbox — Map renderingPrivacy Policy ↗
ipapi.co — Country detection for language & emergency numberPrivacy Policy ↗

These providers process data on our behalf and do not use it for advertising.

3.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect safety.

4. Family Consent System

  • Adding someone as a family member requires their explicit consent
  • Both parties must accept the connection before any data is shared
  • Removing a connection immediately stops all location sharing

5. Data Storage & Security

We take security seriously:

  • All data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with encrypted connections (TLS/SSL)
  • Security PINs are stored as SHA‑256 hashes
  • Row‑Level Security (RLS) ensures users can only access their own data
  • All API communication uses HTTPS encryption

No system is 100% secure, but we use industry‑standard protections.

6.6 Account Deletion

When you delete your account:

  • All personal data, GPS history, SOS trails, contacts, family connections, and profile information are permanently deleted
  • Notification logs are anonymized

6.7 Consent Records (Retained Indefinitely)

To comply with legal, audit, and safety requirements, we retain proof of user consent indefinitely. This includes:

  • user_id
  • email address
  • policy version
  • timestamp of acceptance

These records are never deleted, even if the user deletes their account. They are used only to verify that the user agreed to the Terms and Privacy Policy. We do not use consent records for analytics, marketing, or profiling.

  • Remove contacts or family connections

  • Request a copy of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@scintilladev.com.

8. Children's Privacy

9. International Data Transfers

The Hunter App

Hunting companion & public land navigation

The Hunter App ("the App") is a hunting companion application operated by The Drone Wave LLC, doing business as Scintilla Dev ("we," "us," or "our"). This section explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use The Hunter App.

We collect only what is necessary to deliver hunting area data, GPS tracking, weather forecasts, and safety features.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

We collect the following when you create or update your account:

  • Email address
  • Full name
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Language preference
  • Subscription plan (if applicable)

1.2 Location Data

We collect:

  • Precise GPS location — when hunting GPS tracking is active (foreground and background)
  • GPS breadcrumbs — recorded during active hunting sessions to create trail maps
  • Waypoints — user-placed map markers during hunts
  • Active hunter presence — your area ID is shared to show hunter density on public lands

Background location is used only during active hunting sessions and requires your explicit permission. A persistent notification is shown while tracking is active.

1.3 Photos & Camera

The App uses your camera and photo library to:

  • Capture harvest log photos (game harvested during hunts)
  • Upload firearm photos to your personal gun collection
  • Set your profile picture
  • Save photos to interest points on the map

Photos are stored in private, per-user storage buckets. Only you can access your photos.

1.4 Hunting Activity Data

  • Hunting tracks (GPS trails, duration, distance)
  • Harvest logs (species, location, date, shot distance, weather conditions, firearm used)
  • Interest points (saved map pins with notes and photos)
  • Firearm collection (make, model, caliber — optional)

1.5 Device Information

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging token (for push notifications)
  • Device platform (Android/iOS)
  • App version

We do not collect advertising identifiers.

1.6 Weather Data

We use your location to fetch weather forecasts from Open-Meteo (a free, open-source weather API). No personal data is sent to Open-Meteo — only coordinates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Display hunting areas, seasons, and regulations on the map
  • Record and display your hunting GPS tracks
  • Store harvest logs and firearm data
  • Show weather forecasts and hunting condition alerts
  • Display active hunter density on public lands
  • Send push notifications (weather alerts, hunting conditions)
  • Enable emergency SOS alerts to your chosen contacts
  • Track gamification progress (badges, ranks, XP)
  • Authenticate and manage your account

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information.

3. How We Share Your Information

3.1 Emergency Contacts

When SOS is triggered, your real-time location is shared with your designated emergency contacts.

3.2 Active Hunter Presence

When you are actively hunting on a public land area, your presence (area ID only — not your exact location) may be visible to other users to show hunter density. Your name, profile, and GPS coordinates are never shared with other hunters.

3.3 Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services:

Supabase — Database, authentication, file storage, real-time servicesPrivacy Policy ↗

Mapbox — Map rendering, offline maps, 3D terrainPrivacy Policy ↗
Open-Meteo — Weather forecasts (no personal data sent)Terms ↗

These providers process data on our behalf and do not use it for advertising.

3.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect safety.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with encrypted connections (TLS/SSL)
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures users can only access their own data
  • Photos are stored in private per-user storage buckets
  • All API communication uses HTTPS encryption
  • Hunting area data is cached locally for offline use

GPS Ghost

Developer & QA testing tool for GPS simulation

GPS Ghost is a developer and QA testing tool for simulating GPS coordinates on Android devices, operated by The Drone Wave LLC, doing business as Scintilla Dev ("we," "us," or "our"). This section explains how GPS Ghost handles your data.

GPS Ghost enables developers, testers, and QA professionals to set mock locations, create test routes, and simulate realistic device movement — for the purpose of testing location-based applications, validating geofencing logic, and debugging region-specific features. It is designed with privacy at its core — it requires no accounts, has no backend, and stores all data locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

GPS Ghost does not collect any personal data. No name, email, phone number, or login is required to use the app.

1.1 Local Data (Stored on Your Device Only)

The following data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • User preferences (measurement units, map style, language)
  • Saved routes (waypoint coordinates and labels)
  • Install date (used locally for trial period tracking)

1.2 No Analytics, No Tracking, No Ads

GPS Ghost does not use any analytics services, does not track your activity, and does not display advertisements.

2. Third-Party Services

GPS Ghost uses the following third-party services that may process limited data:

Mapbox — Map tiles and geocoding/directions API (sends coordinates to render maps and calculate routes)Privacy Policy ↗
RevenueCat — Subscription management (sends anonymous device ID to verify purchases; no personal info is shared)Privacy Policy ↗

No other data is transmitted to any server. GPS Ghost has no backend.

3. Data Storage & Security

All user data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. No data is stored on any external server operated by us.

  • We do not operate a backend or database for GPS Ghost
  • All route data, preferences, and settings remain on your device
  • No encryption of local data is performed beyond what the operating system provides

4. Data Retention

  • All local data persists until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • No server-side data retention — GPS Ghost has no backend
  • RevenueCat retains anonymous purchase records per their own policy
  • Mapbox API calls are transient (no persistent user data stored by Mapbox on our behalf)

5. Your Rights

Since GPS Ghost does not collect or store personal data on any server, there is no personal data for us to access, modify, or delete on our end.

You can delete all local data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's data in your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app

For questions, contact us at contact@scintilladev.com.

6. Children's Privacy

GPS Ghost is not intended for children under 13. Since the app does not collect personal information, no data from children is collected or stored.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Your continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
