⚡ Features

Everything you need to
simulate GPS.

📍

Static Mode

Lock your GPS to any point on the map. Your device reports that exact location to all apps — without moving an inch.

🛣️

Route Simulation

Build routes with up to 5 waypoints. The app simulates realistic movement along the calculated path — like you're actually driving.

🎲

Random Mode

Let the app bounce between your waypoints in a random order. Great for simulating unpredictable movement patterns.

⚡

Custom Speed

Set any speed you need — from walking pace to highway speeds. Fine-tune it in real time during simulation.

⏱️

Timer Control

Set a timer and let the app loop your route until time runs out — or adjust speed automatically to fit a target duration.

💾

Saved Routes

Save your favorite routes and replay them anytime. No internet needed — everything stays on your device.

🌍

14 Languages

Available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, and Turkish.

🔒

Zero Data Collected

No account. No login. No data sent to any server. Everything runs locally on your device. Your privacy is absolute.

🗺️

Interactive Map

Powered by Mapbox — search any address in the world, drop pins, or long-press anywhere on the map to place waypoints.
