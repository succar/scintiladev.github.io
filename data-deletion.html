The SOS App

Data deletion instructions

The SOS App respects your right to control your personal data. This section explains how to delete your account, remove specific data, and what happens when you do.

1. Delete Your Account (All Data)

To permanently delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Open The SOS App on your device
  2. Go to your Profile tab
  3. Scroll down and tap "Advanced Options"
  4. Tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted.

Step 2 — Tap "Advanced Options"

Step 3 — Tap "Delete Account"

2. Delete a Saved Place

To remove a saved place (geofence) and its associated alerts:

  1. Open the app and go to the Map tab
  2. Tap on the place (geofence) you want to remove
  3. Tap the delete/remove button on the place details
  4. Confirm the deletion — the place and all its associated alerts will be removed

3. Delete a Trip

To remove a single trip and its route data:

  1. Open the app and go to the Trips/History section
  2. Find the trip you want to delete
  3. Tap on the trip to open it
  4. Tap the trash bin icon to delete it
  5. Confirm the deletion — the trip route and all its data will be permanently removed

Tap a trip to view and delete it

Delete All Trips & Stats

To delete all your trips and driving stats at once:

  1. Go to your Profile tab
  2. Scroll down and tap "Advanced Options"
  3. Tap "Delete All My Trips & Stats"
  4. Confirm the deletion — all trip routes, driving statistics, and related data will be permanently removed

"Delete All My Trips & Stats"

4. What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our servers:

  • Account profile (name, email, phone number)
  • GPS location history
  • Emergency SOS alert history
  • Geofence places and alerts
  • Check-in history
  • Battery alert history
  • Family member connections
  • Saved trips and route history
  • Subscription information (RevenueCat is notified to cancel)
  • Push notification tokens

5. What Data Is Retained

None. All user data is permanently deleted from our servers immediately upon request. There is no additional retention period.

6. Alternative Method

If you cannot access the app, you can request data deletion by emailing support@thesosapp.com with the subject line "Delete My Data" and including the email address associated with your account.

We will process your request within 7 business days.

GPS Ghost

Data deletion instructions

GPS Ghost respects your right to control your data. Since GPS Ghost stores all data locally on your device with no backend or user accounts, data deletion is simple and immediate.

1. How Data Is Stored

GPS Ghost stores all data locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This includes:

  • User preferences (measurement units, map style, language)
  • Saved routes (waypoint coordinates and labels)
  • Install date (for trial period tracking)

No data is stored on any external server operated by us. GPS Ghost has no backend, no accounts, and no server-side data.

2. Delete Your Data

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it by:

  • Uninstalling the app — removes all app data from your device
  • Clearing app data — go to your device's Settings → Apps → GPS Ghost → Clear Data (or Storage → Clear Data on some devices)

Both methods will permanently remove all locally stored data.

3. What Data Is Deleted

When you clear app data or uninstall GPS Ghost, the following is removed:

  • All saved routes and waypoints
  • All user preferences and settings
  • Install date and trial tracking data

There is no server-side data to delete.

4. Third-Party Data

  • RevenueCat retains anonymous purchase records per their own retention policy. This data is linked to an anonymous device ID, not personal information. You can contact RevenueCat directly for their data deletion process.
  • Mapbox API calls are transient — no persistent user data is stored by Mapbox on our behalf.

Need Help Deleting Your Data?

If you have any trouble deleting your data or need assistance, we're happy to help.
