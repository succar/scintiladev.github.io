Privacy Policy
The SOS App

Last Updated: February 18, 2026  ·  Developer: Scintilla Dev

01

Introduction

The SOS App ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal safety application developed by Scintilla Dev. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Your safety is our priority — and so is your privacy. We collect only what is necessary to deliver the core safety features you rely on.
02

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (required for account creation)
  • Full name (required for contact identification)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Profile picture (optional)

Location Data

  • Precise GPS location — collected ONLY when SOS tracking is active or when family live location sharing is enabled
  • Approximate location — used as fallback when GPS is unavailable (IP-based geolocation)
  • Location data is ONLY shared with contacts YOU choose (emergency contacts and family members)

Device Information

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging token — used solely to deliver push notifications to your device
  • No advertising identifiers are collected
03

How We Use Your Information

  • To send emergency SOS alerts to your chosen contacts
  • To share your live GPS location with your chosen contacts during emergencies
  • To enable family location sharing with mutually consented family members
  • To deliver push notifications (SOS alerts, check-in reminders, family requests)
  • To authenticate your account
04

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do NOT share data with advertisers, analytics companies, or data brokers.

Your data is ONLY shared with:

  • Emergency contacts YOU designate (when SOS is triggered)
  • Family members who MUTUALLY consent to location sharing
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) for push notification delivery only
05

Family Consent System

Adding someone as a family member requires their explicit consent. Both parties must accept the connection before any location data is shared. Either party can remove the connection at any time, immediately stopping location sharing.

06

Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with encrypted connections (TLS/SSL)
  • Security PINs are stored as SHA-256 hashes — we never store or see your actual PIN
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensure users can only access their own data and data shared with them
  • All API communication uses HTTPS encryption
07

Data Retention

  • GPS tracking data is retained for historical route viewing
  • You can delete your entire account and all associated data at any time through Settings → Profile → Delete Account
  • When you delete your account, all personal data, GPS history, contacts, and family connections are permanently removed
08

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (visible in your profile)
  • Correct your personal data (edit profile)
  • Delete your personal data (delete account)
  • Stop location sharing at any time (disable tracking or use Hide Me mode)
  • Remove any contact or family connection at any time
09

Children's Privacy

The SOS App is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.
